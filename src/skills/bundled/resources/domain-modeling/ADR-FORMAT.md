# Architecture Decision Record Format

Create an ADR only when there is a real decision, at least two credible alternatives, and a durable consequence.

```markdown
# NNNN: Decision title

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces require a decision. Use terms from `CONTEXT.md`.

## Decision

The chosen direction and its scope.

## Alternatives

- **Alternative:** why it was not selected

## Consequences

- Positive consequence
- Cost, constraint, or follow-up obligation
```

Do not rewrite accepted ADR history. Supersede it with a new ADR and link both records.
